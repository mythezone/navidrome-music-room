# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

Harassment, sexualized language, personal attacks, trolling, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report abuse privately through the repository's maintainer contact or GitHub abuse tools. Maintainers will investigate fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may edit or remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban repeated or severe abuse.

This policy is adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
